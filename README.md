## Requirement Description

### CaRe - Calm and Reflect: Mental Health Journaling App

Your company has been engaged by a local psychologist practice to create a wellness app for their clients. The website is intended to help patients record their experiences and track their activity across a number of days.

The website is intended to be a broad collection of a range of different functionalities located in the one space. The service will primarily be used by the patients, but therapists and other professionals within the practice will also have access. There is also a need to meet regulatory requirements and therefore access by auditors will be required as well.

The client is looking for the following functionalities:

- **Patient Journal Entries**: Patients should be able to record daily journal entries about their emotions and state of mind.
- **Activity Tracking**: Patients should be able to record sleeping cycles, eating habits, exercise, and select from daily affirmations to drive their day.
- **Dashboard**: Patients should have access to a dashboard that shows their activities over the last week and their goals for the current week. They should also have the ability to view records from previous weeks.
- **Minimized Data Entry**: The app should minimize repetitive data entry, such as dates, times, state of mind, and defined emotions, to save time for patients with limited availability.
